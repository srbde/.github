# Sustainable Resource and Business Development Enterprise (SRBDE)

## We build open tools for communities that build things together

An independent software enterprise publishing open-source infrastructure, community-first SaaS platforms, and open creative content — designed to outlast the incentive structures that make most software extractive.

## 🎯 Mission & Vision

- **Our Mission:** We exist to make useful software available to people who need it most. Our goal is to develop, publish, and maintain software tools that serve real communities — not pitch decks. We work across blockchain ecosystems, tabletop gaming, and decentralized data infrastructure because the problems that matter don't respect category boundaries.
- **Our Vision:** A world where independent builders have enterprise-grade tools. We envision a software landscape where individuals, cooperatives, and small organizations can rely on tools that are well-maintained, honestly documented, and genuinely free to use — not locked behind platforms that disappear when the funding runs out.

## 🍃 Our Approach to Sustainability

> "In agriculture, sustainability means returning more to the soil than you take. We apply the same logic to software ecosystems. Open source is not a business model. It is a value. Our SaaS products fund our open work — that is the model. The open work is the mission."

### Our Values

- **Longevity over growth:** We build for the decade, not the quarter. Software that will still work when the hype cycle has moved on.
- **Transparency as default:** Open code, open process, and honest documentation. We do not hide the internals of tools that others depend on.
- **Independence without isolation:** No external investors means no misaligned incentives — but we build in public and collaborate freely across communities.
- **Communities as partners:** Our users inform our roadmap. The people who rely on our software have a voice in where it goes next.
